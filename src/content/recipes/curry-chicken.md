1. Bake marinated chicken till browned, not fully cooked
2. add 500ml water to curry chicken
3. add steamed potato, carrot
4. mix bowl ingredients with prev ingrediens, bring to boil
5. add coconut cream, once boiling, simmer low  for 10 mins
6. serve with rice and cilantro
