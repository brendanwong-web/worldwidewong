1. Beat 6 egg whites with stabilised to stiff white peaks
2. In another bowl beat 6 egg yolks with pinch salt, 5 Tbsp flour, 5 tbsp sugar, vanilla essence, tbsp oil
3. Add tbsp flour tbsp sugar until loose batter
4. Fold in egg whites  
5. Bake at 160 for 20 mins
