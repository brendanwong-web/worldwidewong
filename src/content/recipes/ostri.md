---
title: Georgian Ostri
description: Beef needs to be the right cut to become tender. Otherwise braise
  for 4 hours. Maybe no potatoes cause it makes the gravy too starchy. More oil
  for better flavour.
pubDate: 2025-09-20
ingredients:
  - subtitle: first
    ingList:
      - 1kg Beef
      - 100g Rice
      - Georgian Ostri spices 1 tbsp each
      - Garlic
      - Garlic
      - 2tsp Sugar
  - subtitle: To fry
    ingList:
      - 2 Carrot
      - 2 Celery
      - 1/2 onion
      - "Tbsp Tomato paste "
      - Butter
  - subtitle: To garnish
    ingList:
      - Raw garlic
      - Coriander
cover: ./ostri.webp
coverAlt: alt for bread
---
1.  Marinate beef in spices, salt, sugar, oil
    
2.  Chop veggies
    
3.  Light dusting of flour on beef, sear off in hot pot
    
4.  Add diced onion to pot and fry, deglaze with some flavourful liquid.
    
5.  Add water/stock and bring to boil, lower to lowest and simmer for upwards of 5 hours at 82-87 deg.
    
6.  After beef is pull-apart tender, take off heat
    
7.  Meanwhile stir-fry vegetables to sweat out with butter, fry with tomato paste, salt.
    
8.  Mix vegetables back into beef stock
    
9.  Garnish with coriander and fresh garlic.
