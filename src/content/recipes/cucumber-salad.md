---
title: Cucumber salad
description: cucumber salad but this recipe still needs a lot of tweaking.
  Cucumbers should be brined for 15 minutes and then thoroughly washed and
  dried.
pubDate: 2026-06-14
ingredients:
  - subtitle: Marinade
    ingList:
      - 1 cucumber
      - 3 garlic
      - 1 tbsp chinese vinegar
      - 1 tbsp light soy
      - 1 tsp sugar
      - 1/2 tsp salt
      - 1/2 tsp sesame oil
      - 2 tsp lao gan ma
      - 1 tsp chicken powder
cover: ./cucumbersalad.jpeg
coverAlt: This is bread
---
1. Brine sliced cucumber ~5mm thick with salt for 15 minutes, rinse and set dry
2. combine all marinade ingredients
3. combine,  top with crushed sesame or peanut.
4. do not use too much sauce:cucumber
