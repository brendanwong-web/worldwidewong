## For Tang Yuan

1.  Combine flour with warmed water
    
2.  Scoop out a 1" diameter ball of dough and boil till it floats. Mix into dough and cover with damp towel
    

## For Baked Mochi

1.  Steam sweet potato, mash, combine with butter, oil, sugar, and pinch salt.
    
2.  Add tapioca flour and knead until a dough is formed
    
3.  Form into balls and bake at 180 convection for 15-18 minutes until the bottom forms a brown crust.
