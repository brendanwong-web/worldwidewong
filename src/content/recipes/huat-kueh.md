  - subtitle: Huat Kueh
    ingList:
      - 1l banana milk
      - 750g red sugar
      - 2tsp baking powder/soda
      - 1kg plain flour

1.  Mix sugar into milk, add baking soda
    
2.  Add flour in batches
    
3.  steam for 2 hours
