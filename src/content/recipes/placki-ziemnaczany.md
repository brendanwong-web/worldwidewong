ingredients:
  - subtitle: In a bowl
    ingList:
      - 1 bag potatoes
      - 1 onion
      - 1 egg
      - salt
      - sugar
      - pepper
      - flour
cover: ./placki.jpg
coverAlt: This is bread
---
1. Grate potatoes and one onion on the finest setting into a bowl  
2. Add salt, pinch sugar, pinch pepper, one egg, and mix  
3. Add flour until the right consistency (loose pancake batter)  
4. Shallow fry in enough oil to keep from sticking
