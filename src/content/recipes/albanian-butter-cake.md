---
title: Albanian butter cake
description: This recipe is for albaninan butter cake
pubDate: 2026-04-07
ingredients:
  - subtitle: Wet
    ingList:
      - 4 Eggs
      - 1/2 - 1 cup sugar
      - 1 cup oil or 150g butter
      - 500g flour
      - vanilla
      - 1tsp baking soda
      - 1tsp  baking powder
      - pinch salt
cover: ./bread.jpg
coverAlt: This is bread
---
1. Whip egg whites with pinch sugar
2. cream egg yolks, fat of choice, sugar, vanilla
3. combine flour, baking soda/powder, salt
4. add mixture from (2) into egg whites, fold in flour
5. bake at 160 for 30 minutes
