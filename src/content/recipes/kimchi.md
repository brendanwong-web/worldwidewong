---
title: Kimchi
description: Kimchi from that one youtube tutorial
pubDate: 2025-09-20
ingredients:
  - subtitle: In a pot
    ingList:
      - 1/4 cup or 50g glutinous rice flour
      - 2 cups or 400g water
  - subtitle: To blend
    ingList:
      - 6 garlic
      - 3/4 thumb ginger
      - 100ml, 2.5g dashi powder
      - 1/4 onion
      - 1/2 korean pear
      - 5tsp sesame oil
      - 2tsp sugar
  - subtitle: To finish
    ingList:
      - 1/4 cup or 70ml fish sauce
      - 1.5 cup or 100-120g gochugaru
      - 1 cabbage head
      - 1 Carrot
      - 1 Cucumber
      - bunch of garlic shoots
cover: ./kimchi.webp
coverAlt: kimchi
---
1.  Prepare cabbage head and salt, leaving to soak for min 2 hours best overnight
    
2.  Chiffonade all vegetables
    
3.  Blend all blending ingredients together
    
4.  in a pot over low heat stir rice flour and water until paste thickens, stir in gochugaru, leave to cool
    
5.  rinse cabbage heads, leave to drip dry
    
6.  in a large bowl mix blended ingredients, fish sauce, gochugaru, and flour paste. You should get a thick red mixture.
    
7.  slather your cabbage leaves in the bowl with the paste.
    
8.  place in airtight container, leave to ferment outside for 1-2 days then into fridge.
