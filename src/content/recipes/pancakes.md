---
title: Pancakes
description: "Georgian pancakes, or Machkatebi, are like traditional pancakes but oomfed up, owing to the complexity of flavour lent by the sour cream/yoghurt, and the deeper fry leading to crispy edges"
pubDate: 2025-09-20
cover: ./pancakes.jpg
coverAlt: "Image showing pancakes"
ingredients:
    - first:
        - 2 eggs
        - 3 tsp sugar
        - lime zest
        - vanilla essence
        - pinch salt
        - tbsp oil
        - 3-4 Tbsp sour cream/yoghurt
        - 1 tsp (levelled) baking soda
        - 5-6 Tbsp flour
---
1.  Mix all ingredients in first list until homogenous
1.  Preheat pan with butter/oil (fairly deep to crisp sides up)
1.  Stir in Next step ingredients, batter should be loose but not runny
