# Recipes

## Georgian Ostri

### Ingredients

*   1kg Beef
    
*   100g Rice
    
*   Georgian Ostri spices 1tsp each
    
*   Garlic
    
*   Onion
    
*   Pepper
    
*   Coriander
    
*   Garlic
    

### Steps

1.  Marinate beef in spices, salt, sugar, oil
    
2.  Chop veggies
    
3.  Sear beef off in hot pot, optionally with potatoes
    
4.  Add rice and water and bring to boil, lower and simmer for 2 hours
    
5.  After beef is pull-apart tender, take off heat
    
6.  Meanwhile stir-fry vegetables with tomato paste and spices
    
7.  Mix vegetables into beef stock
    
8.  Garnish with coriander and fresh garlic.
