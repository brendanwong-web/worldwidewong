# Turkey

## Getting from Antalya to Kas
Head to the [Antalya otogar](https://maps.app.goo.gl/fFEDJX4VD4fUeiYD7), the Antalya coach station. From here, ask around for buses heading towards Kaş, the journey should take around 4 hours. 

## Diving in Kaş
- [Nautilus Diving](http://www.nautilusdivingkas.com/)
- [Kas Diving](http://www.kas-diving.com/)

Typically 3 days for Open Water course
