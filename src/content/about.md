I am extremely interested in the future of Mankind. As a city-dweller my entire life, I am painfully aware of the many technologies and systems in place that exist to ensure the daily lives of billions of people continue to run smoothly. Painfully aware because, while I recognise these systems, I cannot say I know how they run, nor can I say that I have contributed to their development in any way. I feel a peremptory calling to address the ultimate exigency of the 21st century; To say that I have adopted and played my part in the development of society in some way, shape, or form.

I have experience working with Siemens PLC and TIA portal utilising both ladder and FBD languages. I also completed a course on microprocessor applications at NUS where I further improved my C++ and C programming skills, as well as gained a greater appreciation for the fundamentals of hardware engineering and integrated circuits.

I speak English as a mother tongue and Chinese as a second language. Having studied German for 2 years before spending 6 months in the south of Germany as an exchange student, I speak German at an intermediate level.  

In my free time, I like to sew, cook, bake, play the guitar, and dabble in photography. I also like to keep fit and play sports like basketball and skateboarding. If anything, I hope that my varied skillset and interests ranging the gamut of hobbies show that I can pick things up quickly and will never back down in the face of hard work, nor pass up any opportunity for learning and personal development.
